Legal & Policies

Please review our guidelines before proceeding with your payment.

Privacy Policy

We collect necessary details (Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Year of Study, Branch, Phone Number, Email, and payment transaction references) solely to process your ISTE membership and event registrations.

We strictly do not sell, rent, or share your personal data. All payments are encrypted and processed securely by Razorpay. We do not store any sensitive banking credentials.

Read Razorpay’s Privacy Policy →

Terms and Conditions

By taking this membership or proceeding with this payment, you acknowledge that you are enrolling as a member of the ISTE GECBH Student Chapter or registering for a specific chapter event, and agree to abide by the general code of conduct of the chapter.

Memberships and event passes are purchased on a one-time payment basis and grant the student access to the respective digital or in-person technical events organized by the chapter.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

ISTE GECBH provides digital memberships, digital certificates, and access to campus events. As such, no shipping or delivery of physical items is involved in any of our transactions.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Once a membership or event registration has been successfully paid for and approved, no refunds will be provided under any circumstances.

Membership cancellations initiated due to disciplinary reasons are at the sole discretion of the ISTE GECBH executive body, and fees will not be refunded.
